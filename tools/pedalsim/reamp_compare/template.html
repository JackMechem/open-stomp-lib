NAM conversion comparison

The same bass DI re-amped through the pedal with the official app's NAM conversion and with open-stomp's (both with the same open-stomp-converted IR), compared with the original NAM model and IR from TONE3000. All takes are time-aligned to the DI and loudness-matched to the reference before comparing.
