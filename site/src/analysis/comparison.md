# Interactive comparison

The full analysis of the [low-end](low-end.md) re-amp session: a bass DI played through the pedal with four
conversions of the Ampeg SVT-VR Classic (Normal) model and the Ampeg SVT Beta52 cab, against the original model and
IR in software.

**[Open the interactive comparison ↗](session2.html)**

It has:

* **Summary tiles**: overall tone, low end, moment-to-moment error, and how often each version is the closest.
* **Long-term spectrum** of every version and the reference, and **the difference by frequency**.
* **Error over time**: how far each version is from the reference in every 100 ms of the recording, against the
  dry signal for context.
* **Heatmap**: where in time and frequency each version differs.
* **Dynamics**, **levels** and **per-band tables**.
* **How it was measured.**

Hover any chart for exact values. The charts follow your light or dark setting, and the button at the top
switches it.
